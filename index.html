What is contextual brand placement?+

It starts with the content itself: finding moments where a product or brand can make sense within a story, rather than sitting separately from it.

Who is Admiro for?+

Brands, agencies, creators and content owners interested in exploring the commercial potential of relevant moments in video content.

Are the examples on this page real campaigns?+

The hero videos are supplied demonstrations. The lifestyle and gaming cards below are illustrative concepts, not claims about live campaigns or results.
